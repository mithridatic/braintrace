## What SP12 established and left

Dose A (3.4968e-4 S/cm2, tau_off 1000 ms) held five of six bands at 200 pA: count 1,
B3's first spike and pre-spike trace unchanged (0.000 mV), mid-pulse p50 within 0.5 mV of
the human, axon-first. The one unmet band is the late level (+1.26 mV). Per 100 ms the
model follows the human within 0.5 mV from 1240 to 1700 ms and then drifts upward
(-66.40, -66.01, -65.62 mV against the human's -67.22, -67.09, -67.00 mV) as the gate
decays. The human's post-spike shift does not decay within the pulse. The comparison
arm (Nap removed) gave no spike at all: Nap is a rheobase lever, not a post-spike one.

The residual is therefore the tail length, not the gate's trigger or size. This spec
changes one registered parameter (tau_off 1000 to 5000 ms) and resizes the density so
that the late level lands on the human's; nothing else changes.

## Dose derivation (from the SP12 traces, no new run)

Doses A and B at the same gate give the late level as a linear function of density:
6614 mV per S/cm2 at the observed late mean gate 0.241, intercept -63.49 mV. The human
late p50 is -67.06 mV. With tau_off 5000 ms the gate after the first spike is 0.483 and
its late-window mean 0.416 (integrated along the SP12 single-spike trace), so the density
that lands -67.06 mV is 3.12e-4 S/cm2 (`s0-ksahp-tail5`). The same line predicts the
mid-pulse level at -67.43 mV (human -67.47 mV).

## Registered predictions

Stage 0 (sweep 56, one evaluation): the SP12 bands, all six: count 1-2; late and mid
p50 within 1 mV of the human; pre-spike trace within 0.1 mV and first spike within 1 ms
of B3; axon-first. Retained trajectory check (reported, not a band): every 100 ms p50
from 1240 to 2000 ms within 1 mV of the human.

Stage 1 (sweeps 50, 53, 43; one evaluation): 250 pA count 5-7 with cycles 4-5 of
250-350 ms; 310 pA count 9-10 with late cycles within 12.8 ms of 120 ms; every sweep-43
onset row within 1 mV of the human and within 0.1 mV of g0-b3.

Rejection: any stage-0 band fails (the family is closed at this gate shape for tails of
1 to 5 s); the 310 pA count falls below 9; a sweep-43 row moves by 0.1 mV or more; the
250 pA count leaves 5-7. The stage-1 prediction is at risk by construction: with a 5 s
tail the gate accumulates towards 1 along the 310 pA train (cycles of 120 ms), and the
resulting outward current is of the order of 0.08 nA. A fall of the 310 pA count is
therefore the informative outcome, not a surprise, and would say that the human's 310 pA
train already contains this brake while B3 lands the rate without it.

Both tiers reported. A pass is a gain-split result for B3, not a promotion; the
first-spike time (B3 78 ms early) is outside this family; sweep 54 stays sealed.
