## Question

The energetic search ([result](../evidence/h01-i-energetic-result.md), Stage F
[decision](../evidence/h01-i-energetic/stage-f-decision.json)) left one row open:
the human refires within 6 to 10 ms of a -79 mV trough at every input; the
finalist (`e-kv3-close2`, somatic Kv3 close factor 2.0 on the candidate) does not.
Somatic Ca_LVA is excluded. The [initiation audit](../evidence/h01-i-initiation-audit.md)
shows the axon already crosses -20 mV before the soma (lead 0.029 to 0.045 ms), so
an absent axonal initiation is excluded too. Two candidates remain on this
initiation pathway:

- **Recovery / availability.** After the deep trough the somatic sodium
  inactivation gate `h` has not recovered when the human would fire again, so the
  soma cannot follow the axon.
- **Post-trough axial drive.** `h` has recovered; what is missing is the inward
  current arriving from the axon (the human upstroke shoulder near -45 mV points at
  an axonal sodium boundary), so the soma sits at the trough until the passive
  return alone brings it to threshold.

One evaluation is in reserve. Stage 0 decides, from the retained traces alone,
which of the two the reserve tests.

### Pre-registered decision rule

Applied to the finalist's soma `NaTg_h` at `trough + 6 ms`, using the median over
the cycles whose `trough + 6 ms` sample precedes the next spike at each input and
the smaller of the two inputs' medians (the rule must hold at both inputs to name
one arm):

| Finalist soma `h` at trough + 6 ms | Reading | Reserve arm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| >= 0.7 | availability is not the limit | **drive**: `--scale NaTg:axon:1.5`, somatic NaTg x1.1 retained in the old slot |
| < 0.5 | recovery is the limit | **recovery**: `sodium_h_recovery_factor 0.5` (recovery tau halved everywhere NaTg is present) |
| 0.5 to 0.7 | ambiguous; the axon already leads | **drive** (same flags as the first row) |

The source rows are a control reading, not part of the rule: the source refires in
5.8 ms at 0.27 nA, so its `h` at trough + 6 ms is what "sufficient availability"
looks like on this geometry.

### Prediction (either arm; the arm changes the mechanism claimed, not the rows)

| Row | 0.27 nA | 0.19 nA (derived) |

### Rejection

- Cycle 2 at 0.27 nA changes by less than the 12.8 ms decision limit (i.e. stays
  above 3.6 ms short of 16.4, in practice: cycle 2 >= 12 ms is a miss of the band,
  cycle 2 within 12.8 ms of 16.4 is the formal rejection of the arm as a drive or
  recovery lever), or
- the loop breaks: peak > +22 mV or cycle-1 width outside 20 % of the human's
  (0.267 ms at 0.27, 0.273 ms at 0.19: i.e. outside 0.214-0.320 and 0.218-0.328 ms).

A rejection closes the reserve: FAIL at cap with the named reassessment (the drive
or recovery mechanism is not carried by NaTg density or recovery time on this
geometry; the remaining family is a slow state the model lacks, outside the
somatic and axonal sodium boundaries). The I cell then stays "experimental,
borrowed, labelled" and Noise1 sweep 48 stays sealed.
