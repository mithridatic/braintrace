## What remains, in order of what a user would feel

1. A cell that passes: the E low-drive gain needs a mechanism outside the passive family (slow Na
   inactivation, Kv7/M, or a second human L2/3 donor with a recorded f-I curve), then one shared
   profile and its transfer gate; the I cell's post-trough burst and accommodation need a slow state
   the model lacks. Until then every simulated cell is borrowed or unpromoted
   ([E close](h01-e-gain/stage-close-decision.json), [I reserve](h01-i-reserve/stage-1-decision.json)).
2. Functional inhibition: no 300 ms arm completed; the 100 ms benchmark (347 s, 2 E spikes) sizes the
   run; the placement question the +0.008 mV somatic result raises is untested
   ([SP5 decision](h01-ie-inhibition/decision.json)).
3. A population larger than 12 cells: the one-point SWC branches that stop 7 of 104 components from
   loading (reader tolerance), then the 40- and 104-cell builds against the derived 19.8 GB, then
   windows beyond 1 ms and the e_only / i_only controls ([40 cells](h01-population-build-40.json)).
4. Per-type donors for the 49 cells without one; both imported donors are type-matched but their
   published fits do not reproduce their own counts here (conditions unsplit: initial state, held bias,
   axon replacement) ([L4](h01-donors/stage-allen-l4-decision.json), [SST](h01-donors/stage-hl5mn1-decision.json)).
5. Connectivity coverage: 157 of 166 export shards unscanned; the 72-candidate pair's merge verdict
   ([summary](h01-measured-connectivity-summary.json), [merge check](h01-pair-merge-check.json)).
6. The 0.19 nA dt requirement for the I transfer (one more halving projected, not measured) and the
   0.19 nA BrainCell train ([SP2 close](h01-i-transfer/sp2-close-decision.json)).
7. Example 21: the adapter contract is a spec; nothing is built.

## What the programme established, and what stays open

In Hartshorne's terms the programme ended with measurements, not a pass, and each search closed
at its cap with a named reassessment rather than a list of causes. Established (necessary and
sufficient conditions observed under registered predictions): BrainCell and NEURON are the same
simulator at a matched mesh and step, so every remaining transfer difference is a time-step
requirement (Y2 closed, dt 0.000625 sufficient at 0.27 nA); on the E donor an axonal sodium
site is necessary for axon-first initiation and somatic SK with calcium decay is sufficient to set
the late rate at 310 pA, while neither Ih density nor leak can rotate the f-I slope at low drive,
which places the controlling mechanism outside the passive family (Y4 narrowed); on the I donor
neither somatic sodium availability nor axonal sodium density controls the post-trough cycle,
which excludes both sodium boundaries (Y3 narrowed); the 104-cell anatomy is identity-consistent,
carries 3 verified contacts after a complete 3,000-sample rescan, and a 12-cell network is
constructible and steps 1 ms with matched controls at a measured cost (Y5 narrowed to a reader
defect and to unmeasured scale). Open: functional inhibition at the pair, the I cell's slow state,
the E cell's low-drive current, 92 unsimulated cells, 157 export shards, the 0.19 nA step, and
every human-qualification claim, none of which this evidence supports.
