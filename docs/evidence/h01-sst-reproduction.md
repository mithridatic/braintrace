## The three verifications

1. **Hoc identity**: `biophys_HL5MN1.hoc` and `biophys_HL23SST.hoc` differ only in the
   procedure name. Every parameter is identical.
2. **NWB**: HTTP 200, IVSCC pipeline 1.0 (SI units stored), specimen 571700636
   (`H17.06.006.11.09.05`), 50 kHz, 57 sweeps; Long Square sweeps 23-37 (-90..190 pA, 20 pA
   steps), repeats 38-47 (40, 50, 60 x4, 100 x4 pA), all stepping 1020-2020 ms.
3. **Fit source**: Yao et al. 2022 (bioRxiv 10.1101/2021.02.17.431698 v5 methods): "putative SST
   (Neuron ID: 571700636)"; three suprathreshold steps plus two hyperpolarising steps were fitted
   (which sweeps is not stated). The SWC header names the same specimen.

## Ready to run (after SP1 releases the machine and the user approves)

See `ready_commands` in the decision JSON: compile the mod files once in the container, launch
`h01_campaign.py --manifest docs/evidence/h01-sst-reproduction-manifest.json --stage 1` detached
via `Start-Process` with a log (cap 4, abort 900 s, wall clock recorded by the runner), then score
with `h01_usable_tier.py --cell SST-L3 --candidate source` and the dt-half repeat. Both tiers are
printed by the scorer; a pass is not required to enter the population.

## Limitation carried forward

The mechanism set equals HL5BN1's, but HL5MN1's soma NaTg shifts (`vshiftm 13, vshifth 15,
slopem 7`) and default Ih shifts differ from the values `h01_pv_rates` hardcodes, and its myelin
has no leak. The BrainCell transfer gate (SP6d) needs those as channel constructor parameters.
